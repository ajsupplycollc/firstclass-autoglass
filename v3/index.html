FAQ

Answers, up front.

How much does windshield chip repair cost?
Rock chips up to the size of a quarter are a flat $80 repair, whether it's one chip or a few. If the damage goes beyond that, a full windshield replacement is usually the safer call.
Should I repair or replace my cracked windshield?
Small chips and short cracks can usually be repaired, which is cheaper than replacement and preserves your windshield's structural integrity. Larger cracks, damage in the driver's line of sight, or multiple chips typically call for a full replacement with OEM-grade glass.
Do you accept insurance for auto glass claims?
Yes. We accept all major insurance carriers and handle the claim paperwork for you. Many comprehensive policies cover windshield repair or replacement, and we'll help you confirm exactly what yours covers.
Do I need ADAS recalibration after a windshield replacement?
If your vehicle has Advanced Driver-Assistance Systems (ADAS) such as lane-departure warning, automatic emergency braking, or adaptive cruise, the cameras mounted to your windshield must be recalibrated after a replacement. We perform ADAS recalibration for all makes and models with specialized equipment.
What areas does First Class Auto Glass serve?
We're based in Mooresville, NC with a second shop in Lexington, and we serve all of Iredell and Davidson County. Come to either shop, or we'll come to you: mobile service at no extra charge.
Do you offer mobile auto glass service?
Yes. We come to your home, workplace, or anywhere in our service area at no extra charge, or you can visit us in Mooresville or Lexington.
How long does a windshield repair take?
Most chip repairs are done in under an hour, so you're back on the road quickly.
How long does a windshield replacement take?
Typically 1–2 hours depending on your vehicle's make and model, plus ADAS recalibration when your car needs it. We'll give you a timeframe when you schedule.
