Privacy Policy

First Class Auto Glass NC · Updated July 2026

What we collect

When you request a quote, contact us, or schedule service, we collect the information you provide: your name, phone number, email address, vehicle details, service location, and (for insurance work) your insurance information.

How we use it

  • To prepare your quote and schedule and perform your service
  • To process insurance claims you ask us to handle
  • To follow up about your appointment or completed work

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the parties needed to complete your service, such as your insurance carrier for a glass claim or our quoting and scheduling software.

Third-party services

Our instant quote tool is powered by Omega EDI, and reviews shown on this site are provided by Google. Those services have their own privacy policies. This site does not run advertising trackers.
